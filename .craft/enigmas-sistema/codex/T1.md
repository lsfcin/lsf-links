## Contract — lib/index.js
- `readFile(env, path, {missing, repo, branch})`, `writeFile(env, path, text, sha, message, {repo, branch})` — defaults `lsfcin/lsf-links`, `main`. `gh()` builds `https://api.github.com/repos/${repo}/contents/${path}`; readFile adds `?ref=${branch}`; writeFile sends `branch` in the body.
